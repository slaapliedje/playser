# Steam store text (draft)

For a Steam store page, if Playser goes there (PLAN Phase 6: decide later,

## Short description

Steam's limit is 300 characters.

> A disc player for your TV, made for Game Mode and your controller. Play
> DVDs, Blu-rays and 4K UHD discs with their menus, in HDR, with surround
> sound sent to your receiver. Free and open source.

## About this software

> **Your discs, from the couch.** Playser turns a SteamOS PC with a disc
> drive into a disc player. Put in a DVD, Blu-ray or 4K UHD disc and pick it
> from your controller: Playser finds the main feature, shows the disc's
> title and art, and plays it with its own menus.
>
> **Features**
> - DVD and Blu-ray menus, including Java (BD-J) menus
> - 4K UHD Blu-ray in HDR10
> - Surround sound decoded, or sent to your receiver as-is: Dolby Digital,
>   Dolby TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio
> - Disc titles, episode lists and art from TheDiscDB, with Play all for TV
>   discs
> - Picks up where you left off
> - Audio and subtitle choice from the controller
> - Made for Steam Input: works with the Steam Controller, Steam Deck,
>   Legion Go and other gamepads
>
> **What you need**
> - A DVD or Blu-ray drive (USB drives work).
> - For Blu-ray and 4K UHD: MakeMKV with your own key, bought separately.
>   DVDs play without it.
> - For 4K UHD: a drive MakeMKV can read UHD discs with (LibreDrive).
>
> Playser is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later):
> https://github.com/slaapliedje/playser
